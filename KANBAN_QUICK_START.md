## 🎨 What You Can Do:

### ✅ View Cards
- Click on any card to see full details
- Beautiful modal with description, labels, checklist, etc.

### ✅ Drag & Drop
- Click and hold a card
- Drag it to another list
- Drop it!

### ✅ Add Lists
- Click "Add another list"
- Type the name
- Press Enter

### ✅ Add Cards
- Click "+ Add a card" in any list
- Type the card title
- Press Enter
